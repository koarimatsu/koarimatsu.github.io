About

Ko Arimatsu (有松 亘)

Astronomer, astrophotographer, and scientific illustrator

Born in 1987 in Gunma Prefecture, Japan

Current Position

Director, Ishigakijima Astronomical Observatory

Lecturer, Public Relations Center, National Astronomical Observatory of Japan (NAOJ)

Research Topics

  • Exploring trans-Neptunian objects and the Oort cloud through stellar occultations

  • Exploring tiny outer solar system bodies through impact flashes on the giant planets

  • Development and application of compact, high-cadence optical observation systems
