Outreach

This page lists selected highlights. The complete list (in Japanese) is available on the Japanese Outreach page.

Commentary in newspapers, TV and news media

TV appearance, “Hoshi no matataki wo nerae! Enpei kansoku de semaru tentai no nazo” (Aim for the twinkle of stars: unravelling mysteries of celestial bodies with occultation observations; in Japanese), Science ZERO, NHK E-TV

(September 2026)

Appeared on a science program featuring stellar occultation observations and explained research results including the discovery of an atmosphere on the trans-Neptunian object 2002 XV93. My artist’s impression was also used in the program.

Press release and press conference, “Outer Solar System Object Has an Atmosphere But Shouldn’t”, NAOJ / Kyoto University / Kyoto Sangyo University

(May 2026)

As leader of the research team, presented the discovery of an atmosphere on the trans-Neptunian object (612533) 2002 XV93 (Arimatsu et al. Nature Astronomy, 2026) at a press conference at NAOJ. The result was released by NAOJ, Kyoto University and Kyoto Sangyo University and covered widely by media in Japan and abroad (NAOJ press release).

Commentary in a wire article, “Astronomers believe they've detected an atmosphere around a tiny, icy world beyond Pluto”, Associated Press (carried by PBS NewsHour, CBS News and others)

(May 2026)

Interviewed about the discovery of an atmosphere on the trans-Neptunian object (612533) 2002 XV93 (Arimatsu et al. Nature Astronomy, 2026). My artist’s impression was also used in the article.

Commentary in a news article, “Atmosphere spotted around small, distant solar system object”, CNN

(May 2026)

Interviewed about the discovery of an atmosphere on the trans-Neptunian object (612533) 2002 XV93 (Arimatsu et al. Nature Astronomy, 2026). My artist’s impression was also used in the article (a Japanese version was published on CNN.co.jp).
